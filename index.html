How it works

Three steps from your wallet to a fresh one. This is the design, and it is not live yet.

Designed so no link shows on chain between the two wallets.

  1. 1
    Deposit

    Your wallet makes the deposit and nothing else. Or skip the wallet and send by hand to a one-time address.

  2. 2
    Batch

    The vault waits for the next batch, trades inside it where needed, and matches coins before they touch a public pool.

  3. 3
    Payout

    A fresh wallet receives the coin. It signs nothing and needs no SOL.

Built with clarity

The rules the vault is planned to enforce, and what it does not claim.

The vault rules

  1. 01A payout needs a signed position. It can be claimed once, to the one address that position names.
  2. 02The vault never signs for more coins than it holds.
  3. 03A deposit that is not credited within 24 hours can be taken back by the wallet that sent it.
  4. 04If the operator posts nothing for 48 hours, anyone can freeze the vault. Each position can then be withdrawn with its recovery code.

The operator

At launch, one party runs the batches. It signs every new root, so you trust it to move balances only as their keys say.

It also sees which deposit belongs to which payout. The chain does not. These rules are planned for launch and are not live yet.

FAQ

Is this a mixer?+
It is meant to be a shared vault that trades. Everyone's coins sit in one place and positions are held by one-time keys, so a deposit and its payout are not visibly linked on chain.
Can I swap without connecting a wallet?+
That is the plan. Pick Send and Tessera gives you a one-time deposit address for that swap. You send from any wallet or exchange in one transfer.
Which coins will it support?+
SOL and coins from pump.fun with real liquidity. Coins that have not finished their curve wait until their pool opens.
What does it cost?+
Fees are not set yet. They will be posted here before swaps open. You will always pay the network fee for your own deposit.
How long will a swap take?+
Each step waits for the next batch. Standard is the fastest. High takes one to four hours and Max up to half a day.
What if I lose my recovery code?+
The swap still finishes, because the operator holds its keys. You just cannot follow it here or withdraw it yourself. Keep the code safe.
